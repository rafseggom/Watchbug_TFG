What it does

Reproduce the failure. Don't guess it.

When a report lands, you have what you need to reproduce it: the annotated image, the console logs and the exact environment it happened in.

  • Tab capture via the browser's native dialog — the widget hides itself so it never appears in its own screenshot.
  • Annotation editor: pen, arrows, rectangles, text and region masking.
  • Bounded console — up to 100 entries with the errors, warnings and logs from the moment of failure.
  • Full metadata: URL path, browser, OS, viewport, resolution, language, timezone and your app version.
  • Feedback without logs: your users can send suggestions without attaching the console.

Privacy by design

Sensitive data dies in your browser.

The SDK never reads your app's cookies, tokens or credentials. And what it does capture is irreversibly destroyed before being transmitted.

  • Destructive masking: pixels are replaced with opaque fill before encoding. No CSS layers that could be peeled off.
  • Automatic from the first frame: type="password" fields, card and credential patterns, and any element marked data-watchbug-sensitive.
  • No host credentials: only your public write key leaves the page.
  • Clean URLs: query strings and fragments are stripped at the origin.
  • TTL-bound lifecycle: identity is purged after 30 days and full reports after 90 (both configurable).

Integration

One tag. Zero build.

No runtime npm dependencies, no blocking the main thread, no breaking your page's CSS. One single global: window.Watchbug. 

<!-- Add this before </body> -->
<script async
  src="https://cdn.watchbugus.com/w.js"></script>
<script>
  Watchbug.init({
    projectKey: 'pb_xxx…',
    appVersion: '1.4.2'
  });
</script>
  • ≤ 45 KB gzipped for the core, with a build that fails if it's exceeded.
  • Closed Shadow DOM: immune to your page's hostile CSS, and no leakage into it.
  • Zero dependencies on the client.
  • Lighthouse drop ≤ 2 points once injected.
  • Also available as an ES module if you prefer to import it.

Actually yours

Everything on your infrastructure. One command.

Three containers — API, panel and database — brought up with docker-compose up. No report ever leaves your server.

  • Triage panel: report list filtered by type, status and project.
  • Lifecycle: Pending → In progress → Resolved, with reopening and internal notes.
  • Multi-project: create projects and rotate their public keys.
  • Backups: volume copy and database dump.
  • Configuration in .env: zero secrets in the repository.

Why Watchbug

Total isolation
