Privacy policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy describes how personal data is processed on the website www.watchbugus.com in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD). It is information provided under Articles 13 and 14 of the GDPR.

1. Data controller

Controllers
Rafael Segura Gómez and Nerea Jiménez Adorna (joint thesis project)
Privacy contact
rafseggom@watchbugus.com
Location
Seville, Spain

2. What data we process and why

a) Browsing data (hosting provider logs)

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. As is technically unavoidable, the infrastructure provider may record information such as IP address, user agent, request time and requested resource, solely to guarantee the security, operation and abuse prevention of the service.

  • Legal basis: legitimate interest of the controller and the provider in keeping the site secure and operational (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Retention: as long as necessary for that purpose, per the hosting provider's logging policy.
  • Recipient: Cloudflare, Inc. as infrastructure provider (processor under its terms of service and the GDPR).

b) Data you send by email

If you contact us by email, we process the address you use and the content of your message in order to answer your enquiry.

  • Legal basis: consent by initiating contact (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and legitimate interest in replying.
  • Retention: as long as needed to respond and, afterwards, while legal liabilities may exist.
The site uses no tracking cookies, offers no user registration or accounts, contains no web forms and shares no data with third parties for commercial purposes. See the Cookie policy.

3. Recipients and international transfers

No data is disclosed to third parties except where required by law. The only provider involved is the site's hosting and delivery provider (Cloudflare), acting as data processor. International transfers associated with that provider may occur, covered by the safeguards foreseen in Article 46 of the GDPR and by the instruments adopted by the provider. See its policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

4. Your rights

You may exercise the following rights where applicable:

  • Access to your data (Art. 15 GDPR).
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR).
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten", Art. 17 GDPR).
  • Objection and restriction of processing (Art. 18 and 21 GDPR).
  • Portability of your data (Art. 20 GDPR).
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise them, write to rafseggom@watchbugus.com stating the right you wish to exercise and identifying yourself where necessary.

If you consider that the processing does not comply with the law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

5. Security

We apply reasonable security measures to protect the information processed on this site (HTTPS encrypted transport, no forms and no sensitive data collection on the web).

6. Children

This site is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect regulatory or functional changes. The date of the last update appears at the top of the document.

Note about the Watchbug product: this policy applies to the website www.watchbugus.com. Watchbug is self-hosted software: the reports it collects are stored on the infrastructure of whoever installs it, and the controller of that processing is the deployer, not the authors of this site. If you deploy Watchbug, it is your obligation to publish your own privacy policy for your end users.
