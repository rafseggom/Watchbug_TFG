## assumption_delta_decision

The assumption-delta detector fired (`detected: true`, signals `pluralization: fallback` / `optional: optional-adapter`); the identity-model question for this slice is the two-layered "image size" nouns in the constant set.

- **Noun now primary:** the **encoded image** — `MAX_ENCODED_IMAGE_BYTES` (2 MB, A-06 "hard") is the contract-level number every client clamp and semantic check targets.
- **Decision:** `add-alongside` — `MAX_IMAGE_PART_BYTES` (4 MB multipart parser guard) remains a distinct constant layered coarser above it (RESEARCH Open Q3 / A5's recommended layered reading).
- **Rationale:** the parser guard bounds work before validation; the semantic cap is what "hard cap" means for CA-01 — merging them would either inflate the contract cap or weaken the parser guard.
- **Accepted debt:** two "image size" numbers coexist in docs and fixtures; e2e must assert 413 at both layers deliberately.
- **What would force a later promote:** A-06 collapsing to a single number, or repeated mis-targeted assertions/bug reports traced to the two-number confusion (watch in `/gsd-verify-work` and Phase 7's closeout).
- Other signals dispositioned `no-change`: "fallback" refers to P1-D-08's contingency ordering (not a data-model noun), and "optional-adapter" is the A-08 v2 `DomSerializeCapture` size-gate lane (explicitly out of v1 scope).
